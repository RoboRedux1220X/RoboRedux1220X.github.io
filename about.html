About

Who We Are

1220X RoboRedux was founded in fall 2025 as the County VEX Robotics team of Prince William County Schools. Our goal is simple: give students the knowledge and skills to navigate in the modern STEM landscape.

What We Do

Inside a Build Season

  • Design & CAD

    We use industry-standard 3D modeling software, including Fusion 360 and AutoCAD, to design our competition robots.

  • Programming

    We program in C++ so our robot can navigate the field autonomously and use sensors to respond to events in real time.

  • Operations

    We manage our own part inventory, budget, and schedule from kickoff through competition day.

  • Engineering Notebook

    We keep a professional engineering notebook that's presented to a panel of judges at every event.

  • Outreach

    We manage our own social media, create videos, and mentor younger students to grow interest in STEM.
